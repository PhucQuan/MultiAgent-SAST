# Tin nhan phan viec ngan cho Quan - Anh - Tue

## Ban gui cho Quan

Quan tuan nay chot giup phan scanner va rule.
Minh chot huong la giu rule Aegis lam nen, roi nap them reviewed Semgrep overlay, khong di theo huong rule tu viet 100%.
Viec cua Quan la freeze 2 profile `semgrep-python-core4` va `semgrep-python-ssrf`, chay lai scan/benchmark, va ban giao 1 bo report JSON/Markdown sach de ben Anh va Tue dung tiep.
Neu con loi nho nao lam vo flow demo thi sua luon, nhung khong mo them nhanh lon.

## Ban gui cho Anh

Anh tuan nay nhan report tu Quan de chot benchmark va gom demo pack.
Giup chay lai benchmark, lam 1 bang ket qua ngan, va viet 1 runbook ngan de ai cung biet chay lenh nao, lay file nao, mo dashboard o dau.
Phan dashboard tuan nay chi can kiem tra flow report -> dashboard co muot khong, khong can code lai dashboard.
Cuoi cung gom lai 1 checklist demo de luc bao cao khoi roi.

## Ban gui cho Tue

Tue tuan nay uu tien day phan LangGraph da lam len repo truoc.
Sau do viet ngan 1 note giai thich so do node: node nao lam gi, luong di nhu nao, cho nao dang la demo.
Khi ben Quan va Anh chot xong artifact thi Tue noi phan node/LangGraph do vao flow triage hien tai de luc demo nhin vao la hieu ngay.
Tuan nay Tue khong can mo them nhanh detector, chu yeu la chot phan flow va cach trinh bay cho ro.

## Ban rat ngan de gui ca nhom

Tuan nay minh chia gon nhu nay:

- Quan chot scanner, rule, report dau ra.
- Anh chot benchmark, runbook, checklist demo.
- Tue push LangGraph, note flow node, noi vao flow triage.

Muc tieu cuoi tuan la co 1 luong demo gon: scan -> report -> dashboard -> triage.
Khong mo them viec moi, uu tien chot cho sach phan dang lam do.
